About the Lab

We turn complex research into accessible experiments.

MoneyBot Lab is an independent AI research studio focused on the practical intersection of emerging technology and human decision-making. We design experiments, publish findings, and open-source tools that make advanced concepts easier to understand and apply.

Our work spans adaptive learning systems, financial interfaces, generative media, and spatial computing. Every paper includes the data, code, or prototype needed to reproduce or extend the work.
