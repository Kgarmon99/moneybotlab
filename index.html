Lab protocol

Every demo has receipts.

  • 1Question. Start with a financial skill that is hard to teach, practice, or remember.
  • 2Prototype. Ship the smallest playable demo that makes the learning loop observable.
  • 3Publish. Share the build, the safety limits, and what still needs to be true.
  • 4Measure. Track clarity, completion, and where the model or design still fails.
Education and simulation only. MoneyBot Labs does not provide personalized investment, tax, or legal advice. The games and demos use fictional scenarios and simplified math for learning purposes.
